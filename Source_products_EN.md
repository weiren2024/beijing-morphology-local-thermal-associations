# Third-party source products

**Table 1. Data sources, periods and analytical uses.**

| Data type | Product and source | Period | Product resolution or input form | Use in this study | Data access |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Land cover | ESRI Sentinel-2 global land-cover product (Karra et al., 2021) | 2024–2025 | 10 m land-cover raster | Identify green and grey foregrounds and calculate green cover, landscape metrics, and MSPA structural proportions | [ESRI Land Cover](https://livingatlas.arcgis.com/landcover/) |
| Building footprints and height | 3D-GloBFP Asian building footprints and heights (Che et al., 2024) | 2020 | Building polygons with height attributes | Calculate building density and mean building height for urban morphology classification | [Asian building data v1](https://zenodo.org/records/11397015) |
| Canopy height | Meta–WRI high-resolution canopy-height product (Tolan et al., 2024; Meta & WRI, 2024) | 2024–2025 | Canopy-height raster; stored in cm and reported in m | Calculate grid-mean canopy height | [WRI product page](https://datasets.wri.org/datasets/meta-tree-canopy-height) |
| Population | ASPECT age-stratified population estimates (Ju et al., 2025) | 2020 | 100 m; persons ha$^{-1}$ | Aggregate all age groups to construct total-population weights | [Product documentation and data](https://github.com/yangju-90/ASPECT) |
| Daytime land-surface temperature | ECOSTRESS land-surface temperature product | June–August 2025 | Nominal resolution of approximately 70 m; summer daytime observations | Match with population distribution to calculate population-weighted daytime land-surface temperature | [ECOSTRESS data page](https://ecostress.jpl.nasa.gov/data) |

Note. Product resolution and the 1 km analysis unit refer to source-data and spatial-comparison scales, respectively. Data-access pages were accessed on 5 October 2026.
